## Findings that change the plan

1. **Don't write "maternal mortality 1,071 → 149".** That line in the report's foreword mixes two measures. 1,071 is the *pregnancy-related* ratio (2000); 149 is the *maternal* ratio (2025). Use one consistent series instead:
   - Pregnancy-related: 1,071 → 172 (2000 → 2025, Table C.18)
   - Maternal: 203 → 149 (2019–20 → 2025, Table 12.5)
2. **The stunting baseline is 51% in 2005,** not 33%. 33% is the 2019–20 value. The full series is 48, 51, 44, 38, 33, 27 (2000 → 2025). Final Report Figure 11.1 has no value labels, so the series comes from the Booklet and is cross-checked against the Key Indicators text.
3. **A full district map is possible** using Table D.4. Stunting ranges from 8.7% in Nyarugenge to 38.8% in Gicumbi, about 4.5 times higher. Every district has at least 150 children measured, which is enough to report.
4. **Mortality rates refer to the 5 years before the survey** (roughly 2020–2025). Label them that way, not as "in 2025".
5. Trend figures print their values, but the extracted text is jumbled. Phase 2 must transcribe them by hand and get both teammates to check them.
6. The Southern Province profile's file name says 2026, but its content is RDHS 2025. No action needed.
