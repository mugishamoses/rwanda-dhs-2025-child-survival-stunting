# Brag plan: From 196 to 36

Source direction: `video/BRAG_DIRECTION.md`. Data: `output/web/data.json` (NISR RDHS 2025).

- **What it is:** a data story from the 2025 Rwanda Demographic and Health Survey for the NISR 2026 Infographic Competition (dynamic entry).
- **Angle:** Rwanda kept its children alive (196 → 36); now one in four doesn't grow, mostly in poorer homes and the North and West, damage happens before age 2, and 2029 is missed at today's pace.
- **Hook:** extreme close-up on a thin giant "196", pull back to reveal the ring of the 2000 survey.
- **Tone:** polished (serious, restrained, long holds, soft fades). Format 1920×1080, 30 fps, 48 s (longer than brag's window on purpose: 7 data beats each need a readable line).
- **Identity (from the project):** sheet #f1f1ef, white cards, ink #1d1d1b, orange #eb6834 (attention), blue #2a78d6 (survival); Archivo (condensed caps 800, thin 200 numerals), IBM Plex Mono labels, Source Sans 3 captions; orange spine strip.
- **Music:** composed calm pad, 110 BPM, Am–F–C–G, drops out at the turn; quiet warm SFX (Kenney `impactSoft`, `click_003`, `bong_001`) on the beat grid. No bundled track (tone mismatch).
- **Share copy:** see `share-copy.txt`.
